Scoring (4/4) Philosophy

The Strength Score is a simple risk rating based on 4 Core Complexity Classes: Each class contributes 1 point, aiming for maximum entropy.

  • 1. Length (Minimum 8 chars)
  • 2. Uppercase Letters (A-Z)
  • 3. Lowercase Letters (a-z)
  • 4. Numbers/Symbols (0-9, @#$!)
Entropy (Bits) Interpretation

The Entropy Score quantifies the randomness and cracking difficulty. The higher the number of bits, the safer your password is:
